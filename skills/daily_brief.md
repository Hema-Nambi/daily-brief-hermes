# Daily Brief Newsletter
Create a concise daily email briefing of news and wellness.

## When to use this skill
- When I want a single daily briefing that can be emailed.
- When a scheduled cron job asks for my daily brief.

## Steps
1. Collect today’s top 5 headlines for:
   - Canada news
   - World news
   - India news
   - AI and tech news
2. For each category, write:
   - A one-line summary per headline.
   - The source name and a link if available.
3. Generate:
   - One short motivational quote (1–2 sentences).
   - One short health or wellness tip (1–2 sentences).
4. Combine everything into a single Markdown newsletter with sections:
   - Canada
   - World
   - India
   - AI & Tech
   - Motivation
   - Health & Wellness
5. Keep language simple and friendly, total length suitable for a quick morning read.

## Notes
- Prefer recent news from the last 24 hours.
- Avoid repeating the same headline twice.

## References
- Use your normal web/news tools to search for current headlines.
